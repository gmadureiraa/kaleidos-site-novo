## Frequently Asked Questions

### What makes crypto go-to-market strategy​ different from traditional GTM?

Crypto launches operate on compressed timelines with global audiences, community-first distribution, and token mechanics that create unique incentive structures. Traditional GTM frameworks assume slower enterprise sales cycles and centralized marketing channels.
