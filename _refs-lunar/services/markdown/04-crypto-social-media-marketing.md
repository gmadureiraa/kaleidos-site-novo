Our crypto social media marketing process

Our process follows four phases designed to build a consistent presence and drive measurable growth:

#### Onboarding & Strategy Workshop

\[\
\
01/05\
\
\]

We start with a deep dive into your project, competitors, and target audience. Lunar strategists conduct analysis, develop your core narrative, and define your tone of voice. We align on KPIs and success metrics specific to your goals.

#### Content Production

\[\
\
02/05\
\
\]

Our team creates content calendars built around your project milestones and market opportunities. Every piece includes branded visuals and strategic timing for maximum reach across global crypto audiences.

#### Campaign Goes Live

\[\
\
03/05\
\
\]

We publish high-impact posts, monitor conversations across crypto X in real-time, and engage where your audience is most active. Lunar handles community replies, amplifies partner content, and capitalizes on trending narratives when they align with your positioning. We track sentiment and identify opportunities to amplify momentum as they happen.

#### Optimization & Reporting

\[\
\
04/05\
\
\]

We analyze performance data to see what's actually working, tracking impressions, engagement rates, profile clicks, follower growth, smart metrics, and reply activity. We continuously refine the approach based on real metrics to improve performance and maximize impact.

#### Reporting & KPI

\[\
\
05/05\
\
\]

We track the metrics that indicate growth and community health in crypto social media management:

**Impressions & Reach:** Total visibility and how many people see your content across the campaign

Engagement Rate: Total engagements (likes, comments, bookmarks, retweets) divided by reach, showing what percentage of people who see your content engage with it

**Profile Clicks:** Users actively seeking more information about your project

**Follower Growth:** New followers gained and retention rates over time

**Reply Activity:** Community interaction and conversation participation

**Smart Reach & Smart Followers:** Quality audience metrics using tools like Cookie3 that show you're attracting engaged, high-value community members rather than passive scrollers

**Content Performance:** Which post formats, topics, and timing drive the strongest results

What you get from our crypto social media marketing services:

Monthly performance reports with clear insights into what's working and what needs adjustment. We show you impression trends, engagement patterns, audience quality metrics, and growth trajectory. Every report includes actionable recommendations based on real data.
